# Free sample: a late-payment tracker for freelance designers

Getting paid on time is one of the hardest parts of design work, and a simple spreadsheet can make it far less stressful. Here is a free starter you can copy into Google Sheets or Excel.

## Columns to track (free sample)

`client, project, quoted_amount, deposit_due, deposit_paid, balance_due, balance_paid, due_date, days_overdue, reminder_1_sent, reminder_2_sent, notes`

## Three habits that help

1. **Take a deposit before you start.** Even 30% changes how clients treat the project.
2. **Set a reminder date on the day you send the invoice.** Track it in `reminder_1_sent`.
3. **Use a calm three-step follow-up.** Friendly reminder at 1 day late, a second reminder at 7 days, and a final notice at 14 days that says when work pauses.
