# Privacy policy

**Who we are:** Paid On Time, operated by Jana Nováková (paidontime.help@gmail.com).

**What we collect:** when you buy, Stripe processes your payment and shares your name, email address, country and the order details with us. We never see or store your full card number. When you email us, we keep the email to answer you.

**Why:** to deliver your order, handle refunds and support, and meet tax and accounting obligations.

**Sharing:** we don't sell your data. Payments are processed by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy). This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. We use no analytics or advertising cookies.

**How long:** order records are kept as long as accounting law requires. Support emails are deleted when they are no longer needed.

**Your rights:** you can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing paidontime.help@gmail.com. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
