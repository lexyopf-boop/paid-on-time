# Free: 3-Step Reminder Ladder for Late Invoices

Most late invoices get paid after the third message, not the first. Send these in order.

## Step 1: Friendly check-in (day 1 after due date)

Short, assumes the invoice simply got lost.

> Subject: Invoice #[number] – quick check
>
> Hi [name], hope you're well. Invoice #[number] for [amount] was due on [date]. Could you confirm it's in your payment queue? Happy to resend it.

## Step 2: Firm reminder (day 7)

State the facts. No apology.

> Subject: Invoice #[number] now 7 days overdue
>
> Hi [name], invoice #[number] for [amount] is now 7 days past due. Please confirm a payment date by [date + 3 days].

## Step 3: Final notice (day 14)

Name the next step. Stay calm and specific.

> Subject: Final notice – invoice #[number]
>
> Hi [name], invoice #[number] ([amount]) remains unpaid after two reminders. If payment isn't received by [date], I'll pause work and apply the late fee in our agreement of [x]% per month.

## Rules that make it work

- One message per step. Never skip a step.
- Always include the amount, number and due date.
- Send before 10am on a weekday.
- Keep a log of every message sent.
